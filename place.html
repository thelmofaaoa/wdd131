About Tonga

Tonga is an island country in the South Pacific Ocean. It is made up of many islands and is known for its beautiful beaches, clear ocean water, coral reefs, traditional culture, and friendly communities.

Tonga has a rich history and strong cultural traditions. Family, community, respect, and hospitality are important parts of Tongan life. The capital city is Nuku'alofa, located on the island of Tongatapu.
